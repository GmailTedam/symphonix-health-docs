Symphonix Health

Logo Brand Case & Usage Guide

1. Concept & Rationale

The Name

Symphonix = Symphony + Onyx (strength). A symphony is many instruments playing in harmony — exactly what Symphonix Health does: orchestrating 160+ microservices, multiple healthcare standards (FHIR, HL7, CDA, X12), AI agents, and clinical workflows into a single coherent platform. The "onyx" suffix conveys durability and trust.

The Signature Element

Source Inspiration

The rainbow ECG/pulse waveform is the heart of the brand — a healthcare vital sign rendered in the full color spectrum. It communicates:

  • Healthcare — the ECG waveform is universally recognized
  • Harmony — the rainbow represents diverse systems working in concert
  • Vitality — a pulse means the system is alive, monitoring, responsive
  • Inclusivity — the full spectrum represents global reach (Ghana, Kenya, Rwanda, Nigeria, UK, Ireland)

Shield Container

The shield silhouette is shared across the entire product family (GHARRA, Nexus, BulletTrain). It conveys protection, governance, and trust — core values for healthcare infrastructure handling PHI.

Rainbow Pulse

The PQRST waveform in spectrum colors is the unique Symphonix signature. No other healthcare company uses this mark. It instantly communicates "healthcare + diversity of capability."

Healthcare Cross

The domain badge anchors Symphonix firmly in the healthcare sector. Positioned at the shield base, it's subtle at full size but clear at icon size — ensuring context is never lost.

Indigo-Violet Primary

Chosen to sit at the cool end of the rainbow spectrum, creating visual continuity. Indigo conveys innovation, wisdom, and technology — differentiating from the sea of blue/teal healthcare logos.

5. Why This Logo Works

  • Instant recognition: The rainbow pulse inside a shield is unique in healthcare branding — no competitor uses this combination
  • Scalability: Legible from 1200px OG images down to 16px favicons. The simplified favicon variant maintains the rainbow pulse at minimum sizes
  • Family coherence: Uses the same shield silhouette as GHARRA, Nexus, and BulletTrain — siblings in the Symphonix ecosystem
  • Meaning-dense: Every element carries purpose — shield (trust), pulse (health), rainbow (harmony), cross (healthcare domain), indigo (innovation)
  • Differentiation: Most healthcare logos use blue or teal. The indigo-violet primary with rainbow accent stands out in feeds, search results, and conference materials
  • Dark/light adaptive: Full variants for both backgrounds ensure the logo never appears as an afterthought on any surface
  • Cultural neutrality: The rainbow spectrum and ECG waveform are universally understood across all target markets (Ghana, Kenya, Rwanda, Nigeria, UK, Ireland)
  • Technical precision: The ECG follows a realistic PQRST morphology — clinicians will notice and appreciate the attention to domain accuracy

6. Do & Don't

Do

  • Use the SVG source for web — it scales perfectly
  • Export to PNG at 2x for retina displays
  • Maintain clear space equal to the shield width around the lockup
  • Use the dark variant on backgrounds darker than #333
  • Let the rainbow gradient speak — it's the signature

Don't

  • Don't modify the shield path shape
  • Don't use the rainbow gradient on anything other than the pulse
  • Don't place the logo on busy/patterned backgrounds
  • Don't rotate, skew, or add drop shadows to the lockup
  • Don't use the light variant on dark backgrounds (or vice versa)
  • Don't scale the lockup below 200 × 60 — use the icon variant instead
