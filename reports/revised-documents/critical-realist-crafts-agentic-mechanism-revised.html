CRAFTS lens4
Critical realist model

Context, mechanism, outcome

Context

  • Patient, encounter, claim, workflow, jurisdiction, tenant, persona, role, and consent state.
  • Available data quality, provenance, terminology, protocol, route, and service ownership.
  • Clinical safety class: administrative, clinical workflow, CDS support, or medical-device likely.

Mechanism

  • BulletTrain assembles and mediates context rather than allowing ad hoc sibling calls.
  • Prompt Engine or Bevan reasons under policy, schema, PHI, and tool-use constraints.
  • GHARRA and Nexus establish trust and route admission for agent-to-agent work.
  • CSAA and HITL determine what may be released, escalated, or blocked.

Outcome

  • A reviewable recommendation, draft, classification, route, proposed update, or approved action.
  • An audit record with provenance, policy context, persona state, and evidence links.
  • A learning signal only if it is generated through approved logs, matrices, or feedback loops.
Mechanism rule. A model output is not the outcome. The outcome is the accepted, rejected, escalated, or audited clinical/administrative action after controls have run.

Bounded intelligence claim

Replace AGI proof claims with bounded network intelligence

Do not use AGI benchmark claims as architecture evidence. The local repositories contain service contracts, requirements matrices, safety gates, integration rules, and implementation code. They do not prove AGI.

Supported claim

Symphonix-Health is designed to produce networked clinical and administrative intelligence by combining specialised services, agents, protocols, and human review under a shared governance model.

  • It can coordinate more context than one person can hold unaided.
  • It can run repeatable checks faster than manual review in selected workflows.
  • It can preserve route, policy, persona, and evidence state for later review.
  • It can fail closed when safety or trust conditions are missing.

Unsupported claim

The repos do not justify saying the platform currently has general superhuman clinical intelligence or AGI-level capability.

  • Bevan is a local LLM/router surface, not an AGI proof.
  • Prompt Engine improves prompt assembly and policy control, not general intelligence.
  • SignalBox provides evidence capture and browser attestation, not outcome truth by itself.
  • Public benchmark claims are external and should be omitted unless separately verified.

Evaluation design

How a realist evaluation should be run

Critical realism is most useful here as an evidence discipline: do not ask whether "AI works" in the abstract. Ask which mechanism, in which context, under which governance conditions, produced which outcome.

Minimum evidence per workflow

  • Real service route, tenant/account scope, persona, role, and permission boundary.
  • BulletTrain-mediated data path with consent, access, transformation, provenance, and audit evidence.
  • Prompt or Bevan route with policy, schema, PHI, and tool-use controls.
  • GHARRA/Nexus route-admission evidence for agent-to-agent interactions.
  • CSAA scope, hazard state, human review point, and release decision where clinical scope applies.
  • SignalBox capture, replay, screenshot, structured assertion, or equivalent runtime evidence.

Evidence that should not pass

  • Scenario-only statements without direct service evidence.
  • Mocked internal services when seeded real paths exist.
  • Empty dashboards, synthetic counters, fabricated telemetry, or unbacked screenshots.
  • Repo-level coverage reused as service-level proof.
  • Claims that ignore patient identity, consent, tenant, or route ownership.
  • Reports that cite public AGI benchmarks instead of local workflow evidence.
CMO record shape. Each workflow should record Context, Mechanism, Outcome, Evidence, Blockers, Residual risk, Human decision, and Next action. That format matches the repo culture better than abstract capability scoring.

Data

FHIR, X12, HL7v2, event, consent, provenance.

Trust

GHARRA record, Nexus admission, JWT/mTLS/DPoP where applicable.

Safety

CSAA scope, hazards, DPIA, residual risk, HITL.

Evidence

SignalBox capture, test result, matrix row, audit log, trace.
